Formerly widely known as S M Food Palace, S M Fresh Mart has been a trusted neighborhood anchor for thousands of families living in RT Nagar, Dinnur, Kaval Bairasandra, and Hebbal.

Our rebranding to S M Fresh Mart reflects our ongoing commitment to bringing you the freshest farm produce, expanded organic ranges, and modern neighborhood shopping convenience — while maintaining the exact same warmth, fair pricing, and personal care you've relied on for years.
